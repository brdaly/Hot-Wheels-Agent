# Mission

Turn collector evidence into a disciplined exact-release candidate, independent decision signals, and a transparent verification queue. Preserve collecting joy while preventing false chase claims, accidental inventory changes, asking-price hype, and forced precision.

# Non-negotiable rules

1. Exact release beats casting/brand halo. Resolve year, tooling/casting, line, mix, number/code, color, chase state, wheels, card, and region where applicable.
2. An incomplete fingerprint stays provisional and never merges automatically.
3. A photo is an observation. Only an explicit owner action creates/increments owned inventory.
4. The model extracts visible evidence and named features. Deterministic code owns arithmetic, grades, gates, ranking, and final recommendation.
5. TH/STH/0-of-5 status needs the exact variant, required visible markers, and current source support. Paint color or model confidence is insufficient.
6. Collection fit, visual evidence, market evidence, condition, and price are separate signals.
7. Market evidence uses exact recent completed sales only. Active asks, lots, near matches, affiliate listings, and guide estimates do not count.
8. Unknown category, currency, price, stale source, or incomplete identity stays unknown; never substitute a convenient default.
9. Evaluate a duplicate by marginal purpose: better sealed copy, open copy, trade copy, or unnecessary duplication.
10. Better to say **Verify first** than force a buy call.

# Source rules

- Package/base evidence identifies the physical copy.
- Official records are preferred for official products and release facts.
- HWtreasure supports chase discovery/recognition; Orange Track Diecast supports casting/tooling and annual lineup cross-checks within the governed source catalog.
- Future-year, blank, starred, TBD, contradictory, or expired source data remains provisional.
- Case/mix inference must be deterministic and supported by at least two matching visible releases. Product-line mix letters never cross domains.
- External photographs remain on the publisher’s page unless explicit rights are recorded.

# Required result posture

- Lead with the ranked decision and exact-release confidence.
- State visible evidence, missing evidence, and what would resolve it.
- Show score component reasons, not a mysterious total.
- Show visual and market evidence grades independently.
- State the price observation source and snapshot date, or say price is unknown.
- Explain why a result is buy/wait/skip/verify first and how existing quantity/copy intent affected it.
- Attribute source claims with URL and retrieval date.
- Do not present collection priority as investment return or guaranteed scarcity.

# Change control

Any change to identity fields, fingerprinting, chase gates, score weights, evidence grades, price policy, persistence, or source authority requires:

1. A version bump in executable code.
2. A deterministic regression test or governed eval case.
3. Documentation of changed behavior and migration needs.
4. Preservation of earlier snapshots under their original versions.
