# Chase Grid image-permission playbook

## Recommended rights path

Start with the **Hot Wheels® Collectors Newsletter**, published by Peter Kistler. Its digital catalog is the strongest
single source for exact-release photography, but its website terms require prior written consent for non-personal reuse.
The Newsletter also states that it operates under a Mattel license, so its approval must confirm whether it can license
each photograph or whether Mattel or an individual photographer must approve separately.

- Publisher contact: `Peter.Kistler@HotWheelsCollectorsNewsletter.com`
- General rights contact: `info@hwcollectorsnews.com`
- [Contact form](https://hwcollectorsnews.com/contact-us/)
- [Website terms](https://hwcollectorsnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Terms-and-Conditions-of-Website-Use.pdf)
- [Mattel permission form](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltc12136c3b9f23503/blt69c76765606e62a6/65dfb0b8aca1711aa89ff4a4/Mattel_Non-Commercial_Permission_Form.pdf)

Backups for publisher-owned photography are [HWtreasure](https://www.hwtreasure.com/contact-us/) and
[Orange Track Diecast](https://orangetrackdiecast.com/about/). Confirm ownership image by image; neither attribution nor
the fact that an image is publicly viewable establishes reuse rights.

## Scope to request

Request a written, non-exclusive license for the named 30-car 2026 Chase Grid, covering:

- worldwide display in the public Daly Ventures Collector web/mobile experience;
- a broader product that may later include freemium or paid member functions;
- self-hosting, caching, responsive resizing, compression, format conversion, modest cropping, and metadata removal;
- per-image attribution and a link to the publisher's corresponding catalog page;
- no merchandise, asset resale, downloads, advertising creative, sublicensing, or AI-model training;
- a defined term or permission continuing until withdrawn, plus a practical takedown period;
- written confirmation that the grantor owns or controls every photograph and can authorize the requested use;
- identification of any asset requiring separate Mattel or photographer approval.

Do not rely on silence, a social-media reaction, an informal verbal approval, or permission that covers only
noncommercial use. Do not hotlink unless the publisher expressly authorizes remote embedding and its bandwidth/privacy
implications.

## Approval workflow

1. Send the publisher the 30-image schedule, proposed presentation, attribution, and public/freemium description.
2. Submit the Mattel permission form in parallel for any Mattel-owned material or trademark treatment that requires it.
3. Record the written grant and exact conditions as immutable rights evidence.
4. Bind each photograph to the exact verified release fingerprint, photographer/publisher, attribution, territory, term,
   transformations, and takedown contact.
5. Create metadata-stripped responsive derivatives and review them against the exact release.
6. Publish only assets that pass the governed-media resolver; retain the neutral placeholder for every other entry.
7. Remove or revoke assets promptly if permission expires, is withdrawn, or the release identity changes.
8. Repeat the process for each new model year unless the agreement expressly covers future releases.
