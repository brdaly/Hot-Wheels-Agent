# Data and source policy

## Source roles

- Mattel/Hot Wheels Showcase and official releases: authoritative public-release identity and collector products.
- Orange Track Diecast: casting history, annual master lists, case/mix reporting.
- HWtreasure: TH/STH markers and product codes, cross-checked for material claims.
- User packaging photos: exact visible identity and condition evidence.
- Approved sold-transaction provider: valuation evidence. Active asking prices are never fair value.

## Confidence

- High: exact release, color, line, code, and chase status confirmed by package details and trusted sources.
- Medium: casting and line are clear, but at least one exact-version field is unresolved.
- Low: incomplete photo or multiple releases match.

## Persistence

- Keep immutable evaluation snapshots and model version.
- Store corrections as new evidence; never silently rewrite history.
- Separate observations, candidates, and owned inventory.
- Store source URL and capture date with every market or identity claim.
